Furnishings

Love your layout? We source, curate, + layer in the pieces that finally make it feel like you.

No construction. No disruption. Just a home that finally feels finished, like it always looked this way.

Styling
& Staging

The final layer for homes that are ready to be photographed, sold, or simply finished beautifully.

Styling is the part most people underestimate. The placement of every object, the layering of texture, the edit that makes a room feel finished. That last 10% changes everything. Whether you're preparing a home for photography, for sale, or just for yourself, we bring the finishing eye that makes all the difference.

This is also the service clients return to after a move, after a renovation, or simply when a space could use a refresh.

What's Included
  • Styling consultation
  • Furniture + accessory repositioning
  • Art placement + styling direction
