Our Philosophy

Home is not a place.
It's a feeling.

We founded 29 Eleven on a single belief, a space should feel like coming home to yourself. One that breathes with your taste, holds your history, and grows alongside the life you're building.

Meet Ashley
Unabashed hat collector. Live music enthusiast.
Lover of all things green.

The eye behind
every room.

Ashley Porter is the Principal Designer and founder of 29 Eleven Interiors. A Newport Beach native, she draws inspiration from the upscale-but-relaxed rhythm of her hometown. Prior to launching 29 Eleven, she spent over a decade at Blackband Design, one of Orange County's most respected firms, where she had the privilege of working alongside an extraordinary team. It was there that she sharpened her craft and developed the design voice that's now her own. Her portfolio includes a cottage at the Lido House Hotel in Newport Beach and features in numerous publications. From coastal contemporary to Spanish modern to earthy organic, Ashley's love of infusing styles with unexpected elements is the backbone of every project.
