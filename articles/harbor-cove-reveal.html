Harbor Cove: designing a coastal home that breathes

Ashley Porter Principal Designer, 29 Eleven Interiors

Some homes whisper. Harbor Cove was designed to breathe. Tucked into one of Newport Beach’s most coveted coastal pockets, this project gave us the opportunity to do what we love most at 29 Eleven: take a beautiful shell + fill it with warmth, texture, + a sense of calm that hits the moment you walk through the door.

The Starting Point

Harbor Cove came to us as a well-built home that lacked personality. The bones were strong — generous square footage, high ceilings, + natural light pouring through every room. But the finishes were generic. The layout felt disconnected. It was a house, not yet a home.

Our clients wanted something that felt effortless + layered, a place where their family could sprawl out on a Sunday morning + still feel proud to host a dinner party that evening. That tension between relaxation + refinement became the design’s guiding thread.

Material Decisions That Defined the Space

We started with stone. The kitchen + primary bathroom anchored the entire material palette — a warm, vein-cut travertine that set the tone for everything that followed. From there, we layered in white oak flooring with a matte, wire-brushed finish that reads casual but looks intentional.

The cabinetry throughout Harbor Cove uses a custom sage-toned paint, hand-matched to complement the ocean light that shifts throughout the day. Hardware is unlacquered brass — chosen specifically because it patinas over time, making the home feel more lived-in with every passing season.

Textiles were sourced with equal intention: Belgian linen on the sofas, a vintage Turkish rug anchoring the living room, + handwoven pendants from a small studio in Portugal bringing warmth overhead.

The Kitchen: Where Everyone Gathers

We designed Harbor Cove’s kitchen knowing it would become the center of daily life. The island is oversized by design — long enough for three stools on one side + a full prep zone on the other. A fluted plaster range hood replaced what was originally a stainless steel box, softening the entire room.

Open shelving in the pantry nook keeps everyday items accessible + beautiful. The mix of ceramics, wooden boards, + hand-thrown pottery on those shelves isn’t styled for a photoshoot — it’s how the family actually lives. That’s always the goal.

Living Spaces That Invite You In

The living room was designed around a single principle: every seat should feel like the best seat. Two facing sofas create conversation. A pair of leather accent chairs angle toward the fireplace. The coffee table sits low enough that feet go up without hesitation.

We wanted every room to feel like an exhale — a place where you don’t think about the design, you just feel it.

— Ashley Porter

The primary suite follows the same philosophy. A linen-upholstered bed frame, flanked by sculptural ceramic table lamps, sits against a limewashed accent wall. The palette is muted — soft whites, warm taupes, + just enough black in the window frames + hardware to give the room structure.

What Harbor Cove Taught Us

Every project teaches us something. Harbor Cove reinforced what we believe most deeply: luxury isn’t about expense. It’s about intention. Every material in this home was chosen for a reason. Every piece of furniture was placed with purpose. + the result is a home that doesn’t just look beautiful — it feels like the family who lives there.

That’s the kind of design we chase at 29 Eleven. Not perfection. Not trends. Just spaces that breathe.
