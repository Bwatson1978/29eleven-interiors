The Project

Mountain modern,
grounded in warmth.

This lakeside home started with a question: how do we honor the bones of a classic mountain retreat while making it feel completely modern — without losing the soul?

The answer turned out to be restraint. We kept the stone fireplace, the exposed wood trusses + the lake views that stop you mid-sentence. Then we layered in everything those original bones were missing — bouclé, brass, slatted wood, thoughtful lighting, + a yoga studio with bifold glass doors that disappear into the tree line.

The result is a home that feels equally ready for a quiet morning with coffee on the lake, or a full weekend with everyone you love around the game table.

We wanted it to feel like the mountains with a fresh perspective— designed with gathering in mind and spaces that prioritize comfort, relaxation + effortless entertaining.
